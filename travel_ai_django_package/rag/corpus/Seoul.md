# Seoul Travel Guide

Seoul is the vibrant capital of South Korea. Key attractions include Gyeongbokgung Palace, Bukchon Hanok Village, and N Seoul Tower.

**Transportation**: Get a T-money card for subway, bus, and convenience store payments. The subway system is extensive and efficient.

**Food**: Must-try dishes include Korean BBQ (samgyeopsal), bibimbap, tteokbokki, and fried chicken. Myeongdong and Hongdae have great food streets.

**Tips**: Many shops are open 24/7. Free WiFi is available almost everywhere. Download Kakao Maps or Naver Maps for navigation.

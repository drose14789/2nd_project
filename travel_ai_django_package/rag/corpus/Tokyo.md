Tokyo quick notes:
- Popular areas: Shinjuku, Shibuya, Asakusa, Ginza, Akihabara.
- Transit: JR + Metro; get an IC card (Suica/PASMO).
- Food: ramen, sushi, izakaya; queue times peak at 12:00 and 18:00.
- Tips: book observation decks, arrive early at temples, and avoid rush hour on weekday mornings.
